Is it worth it?

There is quite a tall learning curve in surfing, and for the average person it could take them a month or two to learn how to surf on a long board, which is thought of as the easier of the two to ride. In comparison learning to ride a short board would take much longer, espeically if you were to do it well. But the effort is really well worth the reward of crusing along the waves and enjoying the beauty of it.
