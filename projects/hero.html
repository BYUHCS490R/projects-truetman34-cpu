My Hero! Joe Quigg

Joe Quigg, a famous surfer that according to google is, "The Father of the Modern Surfboard." He pioneered modern surfing by changing surfboards from heavy wood to fiberglass and foam boards. He also designed what is called the malibu chip.

My first surfboard, which I am still using now was actually one of the boards that he has made. I do love this board and it has made me fall in love with surfing all the more.

Fun Facts

The Malibu Chip
Designed by Joe Quigg, a lighter and more manuverable fiberglass board.
Waterman
Joe Quigg specialized in not just surfing, but all types of transport, paddleboards, canoes, and boats.
FiberGlass
His influence moved board from wooden and heavy to lighter fiberglass boards
