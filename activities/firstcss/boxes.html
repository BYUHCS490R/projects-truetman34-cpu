Box Model

  • Inline tags are like a, img, span. This keeps information all on one line. The other hand block tags give it its own block, like with figure or h# tags.
  • The margin is the difference between the box and the outside, the padding is the box and the stuff inside of it, while the border is the outline of the box itself
  • Pixels, refered to as px.
  • One might use a figure tag when wanting a block and not an inline tag behavior for an image.
